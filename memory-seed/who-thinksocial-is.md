ThinkSocial (thinksocialco.com) is an agency for online boutiques and live sellers. Desiree Rose is the owner and CEO. It is known as an official CommentSold marketing and implementation partner. The work is done by a team of about 33 virtual assistants and about 15 data entry people, organized by role (VA leads, contract management, ads, Instagram, graphics).

**Why:** every agent needs to know who it works for and what kind of client it serves before it routes or drafts anything.

**How to apply:** speak for the agency only when Desiree asks you to. Speak for a client only inside that client's folder, in that client's voice. Never name a team member in anything external without Desiree's yes.
