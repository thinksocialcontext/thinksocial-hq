Drafts, research, checklists, sheets and plans are free. Anything that leaves this computer or changes something live needs Desiree's yes to that exact item: an email, a post, an ad, a budget, a proposal sent to a prospect, a store change, an inventory write, a message to a VA or a client. The request goes in `approvals/`.

**Why:** the agency's name, and often a client's money and store, sit behind every one of these. A mistake in public or in a live store cannot be unsent.

**How to apply:** prepare the work completely, write the approval file, and stop. Act only after its status is `approved`, then record the approval in the ledger. "Looks good" about a draft is not a yes to send it.
