A bare prospect store link means: research with sources (what they sell, platforms, ads running, reviews and customer words), 10 sample ad stills built from their real product photos with a critic pass, a proposal page modelled on the last good one, and a F.O.C.U.S. rundown of their customers' voice (Frustration, Outcome, Constraint, Unspoken cost, Skepticism).

**Why:** the samples prove the approach before a sales call, and the package makes every pitch the same quality.

**How to apply:** Growth Research does the research, read only. Growth builds the ads and proposal in `growth/proposals/<slug>/`. Nothing goes to the prospect without Desiree's yes. Steps in `growth/docs/prospect-package.md`.
