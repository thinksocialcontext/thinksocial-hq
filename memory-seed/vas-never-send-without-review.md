No agent sends anything to a client's customer, a client, or a prospect. Agents write drafts. A VA reviews each draft in Front Desk (or the mail tool), then sends, edits and sends, writes a team note, or escalates. Anything sensitive (refunds, complaints, legal, wholesale, a policy exception) is escalated to the VA lead or Desiree.

**Why:** a draft is cheap to fix; a sent email in a client's name is not.

**How to apply:** every inbox prompt and SOP says draft only. Never give an agent a send tool. Training for VAs is in `team/sops/front-desk.md`.
