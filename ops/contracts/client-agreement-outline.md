# Client agreement outline

* Parties, start date, term and renewal
* Services, matching the client's `profile.md` services list
* What the client provides: access through 1Password, brand files, a decision maker, response times
* Approvals: nothing is published, sent, launched or written to the store without the client's agreed approver
* Fees, billing day, late payment, ad spend paid by the client directly
* Data: the client owns their data; ThinkSocial keeps it private and deletes it on request when the agreement ends
* Ending the agreement: notice period, handover of files and access
