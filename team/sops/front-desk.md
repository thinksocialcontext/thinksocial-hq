# Front Desk: how VAs work the inbox queue

Front Desk is the review queue where every client's inbox agent leaves its drafts. The agent never sends. You do, after you read.

## For each item in the queue

1. **Read the customer's email first, then the draft.** Then read the internal note beside the draft: the sources the agent used, its confidence, and what it says to check.
2. **Pick one of four actions.**
   * **Send** when the draft is right as written.
   * **Edit and send** when it needs a change. Your edit teaches the knowledge base, so fix the facts, not only the wording.
   * **Team note** when something is worth recording for the next VA but needs no decision: "customer already emailed twice about this order", "owner prefers we offer an exchange first". A note does not move the item to anyone.
   * **Escalate** when a person above you must decide: refunds and disputes, an angry customer, a legal or chargeback threat, wholesale or press, any exception to policy, anything the knowledge base does not cover. Say in one line what decision is needed. Escalating is not a failure; guessing is.
3. **Action needed items** (a damaged or wrong order) cannot be sent until you have done the fix in the store, or asked the person who can, and ticked Action completed.

## Note or escalate?

| Situation | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| A fact the next VA should know, no decision needed | Team note |
| You need someone to decide or approve | Escalate |
| The draft is wrong because the knowledge base is wrong | Edit and send, then a team note saying what was wrong |
| You are unsure | Escalate |

## No holding replies by default

Do not send "we got your message and will get back to you" replies unless the client's profile says `holding_replies: yes`. If you cannot answer yet, escalate and leave the email for the real answer.

## Never

* Never send from the client's mailbox outside Front Desk or the agreed mail tool.
* Never paste a password or a customer's card or address details into a note.
* Never promise a refund, a delivery date or a restock the knowledge base does not support.
